这篇文章的实验是在GR00T N1.5和VGGT上实验的，因此结论可能和特定模型相关。
但是我个人人为它很诚实的一个结论是：
> Task-level finetuning of geometric VLAs combining GR00T-N1.5 and VGGT does not lead to a statistically significant increase in the success rate. The Early Fusion geometric VLA appears to be more promising in real world experiments.
